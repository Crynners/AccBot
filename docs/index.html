Stack sats on autopilot

AccBot buys Bitcoin for you on a schedule, straight from your own exchange account. It runs on your phone, so there's no cloud service and no middleman taking a cut.

View on GitHub

Your keys stay with you

API keys never leave your phone. They're encrypted with AES-256-GCM in the Android Keystore. Nothing goes to the cloud and nothing is tracked.

Set it and forget it

Set up a plan once and AccBot keeps buying on schedule, even while your phone sits in your pocket.

Open source and free

The code is public under the MIT license. No subscription, no premium tier, no data collection. You only pay your exchange's usual trading fee.

Frequently asked questions

Is AccBot really free?

Yes. AccBot is open source under the MIT license and costs nothing. There's no subscription and no extra fee on your purchases. You only pay your exchange's normal trading fee.

Can AccBot withdraw or lose my money?

AccBot only works with the API key you create on your exchange. Give the key permission to trade and leave withdrawals turned off. The key is stored encrypted on your phone, and AccBot only ever sends it to your exchange.

Does my phone need to be on?

Yes. Purchases run on your phone, so it needs to be switched on and online. Android sometimes delays background tasks by a few minutes. If a purchase is missed, for example while the phone was off, AccBot shows it on the dashboard and lets you buy it later or skip it.

Where are my coins kept?

Purchased coins land in your exchange account. AccBot can remind you when your balance reaches a limit you choose, so you can move it to your own wallet.

Which coins and currencies are supported?

You can buy BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, DOT, LTC and BNB with EUR, USD, GBP, CZK, USDT or USDC. What's available depends on your exchange.

Is there an iPhone version?

The iOS app is in the works. For now AccBot is available for Android on Google Play and GitHub.

Can I try it without real money?

Yes. Turn on sandbox mode and AccBot trades on the Binance spot testnet with test funds.
